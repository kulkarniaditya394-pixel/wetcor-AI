5. Methods and limitations

  • Contact angle: OpenCV segmentation, specimen baseline (automatic substrate fit or operator-placed), sub-pixel boundary, local rotated-frame polynomial tangent per side and spherical-cap fit. Classification: below 90 deg hydrophilic, above 90 deg hydrophobic, exactly 90 deg boundary.
  • Current density i = I / A with the recorded exposed area. R_p = dE/di near E_corr. i_corr = B / R_p only with a documented B. Tafel: E = a + beta log10|i| on user-confirmed regions, intersection gives i_corr and E_corr. CR = 0.00327 i_corr EW / rho (mm/y; uA/cm2, g/eq, g/cm3).
  • OCP and EIS results are not converted into corrosion rates.
  • No compliance with any test standard is claimed. Synthetic-image accuracy of the contact-angle software is not a real-world metrology accuracy.
