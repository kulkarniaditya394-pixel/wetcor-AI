# Contact-angle validation images

Openly licensed images whose contact angle was published by their authors. They are used only to measure the detector
(`scripts/evaluate_contact_angle.py --real`) and are not experimental data of this project.

The labels are the authors' own readings, made with their own instruments or software, so they are a comparison and not
ground truth. `labels.json` lists the author, licence and source page of every file. The 18 `glass_panel_*` files are crops
of one published table and the goniometer screenshot is a crop; adapted under the same licence.
